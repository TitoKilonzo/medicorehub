Our Mission

Healthcare Data Should Work For Clinicians — Not Against Them

Fragmented records. Offline systems. Paper registers at the point of care. These are not abstractions — they cost lives. MediCore Hub was engineered specifically for Kenya and East Africa's health system reality: low bandwidth, multi-site facilities, and a critical shortage of clinical staff.

We replace silos with a single source of truth — accessible from a browser, offline-capable, and built on open standards that integrate with NHIF, KHIS, and national health registries.

Offline-First Architecture

Works in low-connectivity environments. Syncs when connectivity returns.

Open Standards Integration

HL7 FHIR R4, OpenHIE, and SNOMED CT built in from the ground up.

Designed for All Cadres

From CHWs in the field to specialist consultants at referral hospitals.
