Eight deeply integrated modules designed for East Africa's health system infrastructure — from Level 2 dispensaries to national referral hospitals.

Module 01

Electronic Health Records

A complete longitudinal patient record that follows the patient across every facility, every encounter, every cadre — structured to Kenya's standard MOH health record formats.

  • Structured SOAP clinical notes with voice-to-text support in Swahili and English
  • Problem lists, allergy records, immunisation history, and growth monitoring
  • MOH 240, 257, 333, and 405 form templates built in
  • Cross-facility record linkage via national patient identifier
  • Offline-first with full synchronisation on connectivity restoration
  • Configurable clinical decision support and protocol alerts
Request EHR Demo

Module 02

Laboratory Information System

From specimen collection to result delivery — MediCore Hub's LIS eliminates paper-based lab workflows, reduces errors, and gets critical results to clinicians in real time.

  • Barcoded specimen tracking from collection to result
  • Analyser interfaces for common point-of-care devices
  • Critical value alerts pushed to the ordering clinician immediately
  • Turnaround time monitoring and QC management
  • NHIF-compliant result formatting for claim submission
  • Reflex testing rules and delta checks configured per facility
Request LIS Demo

Module 03

Pharmacy Management

Close the loop between prescribing and dispensing with a pharmacy module that enforces safe medication practices and keeps stock levels optimised against the national essential medicines list.

  • Electronic prescribing with real-time drug-drug interaction alerts
  • Formulary management aligned to KEML and facility tier
  • Dispensing verification and patient counselling prompts
  • Narcotic and controlled substance audit trail
  • Automated reorder triggers linked to KEMSA requisition workflows
  • Expiry management with near-expiry alerts and rotation reports
Request Pharmacy Demo

Module 04

Radiology & PACS

A web-based DICOM viewer and radiology order management system that eliminates costly workstations and enables remote reporting by specialists anywhere in Kenya.

  • Zero-footprint web DICOM viewer — works in any modern browser
  • Integrated radiology order entry from the clinician's EHR view
  • Structured reporting templates for common modalities
  • Teleradiology workflow for specialist review at remote sites
  • DICOM conformance with all major X-ray, ultrasound, and CT equipment
  • Automated prior study comparison and hanging protocols
Request Radiology Demo

Module 05

Scheduling & Appointments

Reduce no-shows, eliminate double-bookings, and give patients the convenience of SMS appointment management — all connected directly to the clinical record.

  • Multi-resource scheduling — clinicians, rooms, and equipment
  • Automated SMS reminders in Swahili and English via AfricasTalking
  • No-show prediction using machine learning on historical patterns
  • Online patient self-booking portal with NHI verification
  • Waitlist management and slot optimisation
  • Follow-up scheduling auto-generated from discharge summary
Request Scheduling Demo

Module 06

Revenue Cycle Management

From registration to final claim settlement — MediCore Hub's RCM module maximises revenue recovery through automated NHIF pre-authorisation and SHA claims processing.

  • Real-time NHIF eligibility verification at point of registration
  • SHA claims auto-generated from clinical documentation
  • M-Pesa, insurance, and cash payment processing in one workflow
  • Denial management with root cause coding and resubmission workflows
  • Financial dashboards with payer mix analysis and AR ageing
  • KRA ETR integration for compliant receipt generation
Request RCM Demo

Module 07

Telemedicine

Extend specialist care to underserved facilities without requiring patients to travel hundreds of kilometres. Every consultation is recorded and attached to the patient's permanent record.

  • Encrypted, low-bandwidth video consultations optimised for 3G networks
  • Asynchronous store-and-forward messaging with image and document sharing
  • E-prescribing with digital signature for remote prescriptions
  • Specialist referral routing with warm handoff protocols
  • Consultation summaries auto-appended to the EHR encounter
  • Scheduling and NHIF billing fully integrated with the RCM module
Request Telehealth Demo

Module 08

Supply Chain & Inventory

Stockouts cost lives. MediCore Hub's supply chain module gives facility managers real-time visibility into stock levels across every store — and automates replenishment before shortages occur.

  • Real-time stock ledger updated from pharmacy dispensing and lab consumption
  • Expiry tracking with first-expiry-first-out rotation enforcement
  • Consumption-based demand forecasting with seasonal adjustments
  • KEMSA integration for automated pull requisition submission
  • Multi-store management: pharmacy, ward, theatre, and central stores
  • Procurement workflow with GRN, invoice matching, and supplier management
