MediCore Hub is built on open international standards and integrates natively with Kenya's national health infrastructure — NHIF, KHIS, KEMSA, and SHA.

Technical Standards

Built on Open Global Standards

HL7 FHIR R4

All clinical data exposed and consumed via FHIR R4 RESTful APIs, enabling plug-and-play integration with any compliant system.

OpenHIE Architecture

Implements the OpenHIE component framework — Client Registry, Provider Registry, Facility Registry, and Shared Health Record.

SNOMED CT & ICD-10

Clinical terminology mapped to SNOMED CT with ICD-10-CM/PCS coding for diagnoses, procedures, and problem lists.

HL7 v2.x Messaging

Bidirectional HL7 v2.x ADT, ORM, and ORU messages for legacy system integration — no rip-and-replace required.

Insurance

Real-time eligibility verification, pre-authorisation, electronic claim submission, and remittance reconciliation — fully automated from the clinical workflow.

Surveillance

Supply Chain
