Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How long does implementation take?

A standard deployment for a single-site health centre typically takes 4 – 6 weeks from contract signing to go-live. Multi-site county rollouts are phased over 3 – 6 months with dedicated project management support.

Does it work without reliable internet?

Yes. MediCore Hub is offline-first by architecture. The system runs fully on a local server at your facility and synchronises with the cloud when connectivity is available. We have successful deployments in ASAL counties with minimal connectivity.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing is a monthly subscription per facility, tiered by facility level and number of active users. We offer government and NGO pricing tiers. Contact us for a tailored quote — we do not publish pricing publicly as facilities vary significantly.

Can you migrate our existing data?

Yes. We have migrated data from OpenMRS, KenyaEMR, Daktari, and custom spreadsheet systems. All migrations are performed by our data engineering team with zero data loss guaranteed in writing.

What training is included?

All deployments include role-based training for every cadre: CHWs, nurses, clinicians, pharmacists, lab technicians, and administrators. We train the trainers and provide a full e-learning library in Swahili and English.

Where is our data hosted?

Data is hosted in AWS Nairobi (af-south-1) by default, keeping it within Kenya's jurisdiction in compliance with the Data Protection Act 2019. On-premise deployment options are available for county governments requiring full data sovereignty.
