From Decision to Go-Live

Your Implementation Journey

A structured, low-disruption rollout managed end-to-end by our implementation team — so your clinical staff can focus on patients, not technology.

01
Week 1 — 2

Discovery & Scoping

Our implementation manager visits your facility, maps existing workflows, identifies integration touchpoints (NHIF, KEMSA, lab equipment), and produces a written deployment plan with a fixed go-live date.

Workflow audit report Integration map Fixed go-live date
02
Week 2 — 3

Data Migration & Configuration

We migrate your existing patient records from OpenMRS, KenyaEMR, paper registers, or any legacy system. The platform is configured to your facility's MOH tier, service lines, and formulary — all before any staff training begins.

Zero-loss data migration Facility-specific configuration Formulary & NHIF setup
03
Week 3 — 4

Role-Based Staff Training

Dedicated half-day sessions per cadre: clinicians, nurses, pharmacists, lab technicians, and administrators. Training is in Swahili and English, delivered on-site or via telehealth. Champion users are identified and trained as internal trainers.

On-site training per cadre Swahili & English materials Champion user programme
04
Week 5 — 6

Parallel Run & Go-Live

A supervised parallel run period ensures confidence before cutover. On go-live day, an implementation engineer is on-site for the first 48 hours. Critical support SLA (1-hour response) begins from Day 1.

48-hour on-site go-live support 1-hour critical SLA from Day 1 30-day hypercare period
